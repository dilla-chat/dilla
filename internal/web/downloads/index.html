Moving from this browser?

Keep your existing browser device and saved 52-character recovery key. Sign in on desktop, complete your second factor, then enter that recovery key to enrol the new device. Your password alone cannot unlock your keys on a new device.

Messages sent before a new device joins are not shown on it. Keep this browser until you have checked your desktop account and the conversations you need. Browser data stays here; it is not copied into Dilla desktop.

If you do not have your saved recovery key, keep this browser device. This page cannot recover or export it.
