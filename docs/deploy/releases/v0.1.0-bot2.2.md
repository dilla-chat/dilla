# dilla v0.1.0-bot2.2

A fix release on top of [v0.1.0-bot2.1](v0.1.0-bot2.1.md). It fixes a Bot that goes silent when its helper stops, a
music queue that kept tracks that could not play, and a voice call that stayed open on a second device. It also makes
`dilla.db` give back free space. The release includes `dillad` with the web client, the Bot and its helper. Desktop
0.1.0-beta.4 stays current: there is no new desktop app yet.

**This release affects every instance, with or without the Bot.** Voice takeover is a server change: joining voice on
a second device moves you there. The first start after the upgrade also compacts `dilla.db` once. If you run the Bot,
upgrade `dillad` and the Bot together. The installers do this.

## What's fixed

- **The Bot no longer goes silent after its helper stops.** A command the Bot cannot answer now shows "The Bot didn't
  answer" with **Retry** after about 5 seconds. A command that waits for a new helper now asks for it at once, instead
  of waiting up to 10 seconds for the next check. If the new helper is up in time, the answer arrives and the command
  works.
- **A pass error no longer closes a working helper.** The Bot closes a helper only when that helper is unusable, or its
  community is gone.
- **The restart backoff no longer resets too early.** After a helper stops, the Bot waits longer before each new start.
  A replacement that served only briefly used to reset that wait. It now resets after 60 seconds of service.
- **Music keeps the right tracks.**
  - A track whose own playback broke is removed from the queue, and the Bot says so. That covers YouTube refusing
    that one video, the helper stopping under the track, the Bot still missing from the call after one rejoin, the call
    falling behind, and a failure to encode or send the audio.
  - A track you paused stays in the queue, paused. So does a track that stopped because nobody was in the channel,
    because YouTube as a whole was unavailable, or because the connection expired or dropped.
  - A track also stays, paused, when the Bot was not allowed to play at that moment: a Bot permission change in the
    channel, a server that was not ready, a member whose devices were still being checked, or a brief server error.
    Press play to try it again.
  - A new `/play` still goes last in the queue.
  - A pass error no longer replaces the helper under a playing track. A track whose helper stops now ends at once,
    instead of after 6 seconds, and is removed. The removal holds across the helper restart: the next `/play` plays
    the new track, not the one that failed.
  - A start whose call membership was just removed now rejoins once and tries again.
- **Joining voice on a second device moves you there.** Your other devices leave voice once the new one is connected.
  Until the next desktop app, the device that left says "You went AFK on another device." In a call outside any
  community it shows the generic removal message. It does not rejoin.
- **`dillad doctor` tells you when commands fail.** It shows a `bot_health` warning as soon as commands fail, with the
  count. The warning stays until a probe after the failures passes. Ordinary restarts do not trigger it.
- **`dilla.db` gives back free space.** The file used to keep every page it had ever grown to. It now reclaims free
  pages, and shrinks once at the first start after the upgrade (see Operators).
- **YouTube: nothing changed.** YouTube refused one request with a 403. It could not be reproduced:
  replays of the same video, through the Bot's sandbox and directly, all played. This release does not change yt-dlp.
  It stays at 2026.10.21.

## Upgrade

1. Take a backup with `dillad backup`.
2. Run `install.sh --upgrade`, then `install-bot.sh --upgrade`, in one window. Both restart `dilla.service`. There is
   no separate step to restart the Bot. Use the installers: they upgrade `dillad` and the Bot together.
3. If you marked a Bot channel while the helper was down on bot2.1, open **Server settings → Bot**. Press **Add the Bot
   again** on each channel that shows "needs adding again".
4. Check that the Bot answers. Run `/reminders list` in a Bot channel, and look for `Commands are working` in
   **Server settings → Bot**. Then run `dillad doctor`. It should show no `bot_health` warning a minute later.

The first start after the upgrade compacts `dilla.db` before `dillad` serves. See Operators for how long that takes.

## Operators

New and changed lines in `journalctl -u dilla`. None of them contains message content, keys, track titles or links.

- **`bot native helper closed`** carries `reason`, and also `operation`, `error_class`, `kind` and `exit`. It replaces
  `bot native helper stopped`. The reasons are `retained_unusable`, `pass_unretained`, `lifecycle_bind`,
  `start_dropped`, `manager_restart`, `invalidated_decode`, `transfer_invalid`, `outcome_unknown`,
  `shutdown_timeout`, `shutdown` and `installation_gone`. `pass_unready` and `music_restore_failed` are reserved.
- **`bot native helper gone`** is logged once per helper, followed by the respawn lines. It replaces
  `helper client is closed` every 10 seconds.
- **`bot native helper respawn requested`** carries `installation_id` and `after`, the backoff it waited. The first
  ask after a helper stops has `after=0s`; later ones wait 10 s, 20 s and so on, up to 5 minutes.
- **`bot command waiting for a replacement helper`** and **`bot command answer waiting for a replacement helper`**
  mean a command, or its answer, is waiting up to 4 seconds for a new helper. The answer line carries `seq` and
  `error_class`. The first wait also asks for the new helper, through the same respawn backoff.
- **`error_class`** is now on `bot command open failed`, `answer not sealed`, `answer not delivered`,
  `text command intake deferred` and `installation reconciliation failed`. Before bot2.2, `bot command open failed`
  always said `class=helper_error`. That value classified nothing. Read old journals with that in mind.
- **`bot joined Bot channel group`** and **`bot Bot channel join failed`** report each Bot channel join. The failure
  line carries `phase` and `error_class`.
- **Music lines** carry `item_id`, a random queue-item id that is never derived from the track, and `restored`. The
  refused line uses `queued_earlier`. `bot music call group check failed` is new and carries `attempt`, `error_class`
  and `stage`. `media_epoch` lines carry `epoch_reason`.
- **`bot music failed track removed after helper restart`** carries `item_id`. A track failed under a helper that
  stopped, so its removal could not be saved then. The new helper removes it before anything plays. If that removal
  fails too, `bot music failed track not removed after helper restart` carries `item_id` and `error_class`, and the
  next restart tries again.
- **`compacting the database`** and **`database compacted`** carry page counts, `file_bytes_before`,
  `file_bytes_after` and the duration. The compaction runs once, on the first start after the upgrade, before `dillad`
  serves. On a 200 MB file that was 98 % free it took milliseconds. With more live data it takes longer, within the
  300 second start budget.
- **`database free pages returned`** carries `pages`. Every hour `dillad` gives up to 1000 free pages back to the
  filesystem. The line appears only when pages were returned. **`incremental vacuum failed`** carries `err`; the next
  hour tries again.
- **Voice takeover lines**, on every instance:
  - `voice moved to the user's newest device; an older one left its call` carries `user`, `device`, `cut_device` and
    `room`.
  - `removing a device whose voice moved to a newer one failed; it keeps its pending reason, and maintenance retries
    the cut` carries the same ids and `err`.
  - `retried a voice takeover removal; it went through`,
    `retrying a voice takeover removal failed; the next maintenance pass tries again` and
    `a voice takeover removal failed on every retry; giving up, the older device may stay in its call` report the
    retries, with `device`, `room` and `attempt` (`attempts` on the last one). The two failure lines also carry `err`.
  - `reading a joined device's user for voice takeover failed; nothing is taken over`,
    `listing a user's devices for voice takeover failed; nothing is taken over` and
    `listing the SFU rooms for voice takeover failed; only the devices it listed are compared` are warnings when a
    read fails.

## Versions and compatibility

| Component | This release | bot2.1 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Server database schema | 58 | 58 |
| Negotiated wire version | 6 | 6 |
| Desktop app | 0.1.0-beta.4 | 0.1.0-beta.4 |

- The Bot's health report gains an optional field. Upgrade `dillad` and the Bot together, and roll back both together.
  The installers do this.
- After a rollback, an older `dillad doctor` may show `bot_health` as "malformed" until `dillad` rewrites the file.
- There is no schema change and there are no new migrations. Wire 6 is unchanged, apart from a new value of the AFK
  pending reason: 3, "taken over".
- Rolling back to bot2.1 is a binary rollback. The database stays at `auto_vacuum=INCREMENTAL`, which older SQLite
  reads without trouble.

## Known limits

- The desktop notice for a voice takeover comes with the next desktop app.

The rest is the same as [bot2](v0.1.0-bot2.md#known-limits).
